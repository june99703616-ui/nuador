La Maison

Born in Paris.
Made to travel the world.

Nuador was founded in Paris with a simple conviction: the pleasure of hookah should not require nicotine or tobacco. We build our paste on a vegetable-glycerin base, infused with cold-extracted fruit, tea and botanical essences.

The result is a dense, aromatic cloud with a soft, lingering finish — crafted to the standards of a French maison, and designed for lounges, homes and curious palates worldwide.

— Nuador, Maison fondée à Paris
